Monhinga

Ingredients

  • Rice noodles
  • Catfish or white fish
  • Lemongrass
  • Garlic, onion, ginger
  • Fish sauce
  • Chili flakes
  • Banana stem (optional)

Instructions

  1. Boil the fish until soft, then shred it.
  2. Sauté garlic, onion, ginger, and lemongrass.
  3. Add shredded fish and broth.
  4. Simmer until thick and fragrant.
  5. Serve over rice noodles with chili and coriander.
